# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in this project to be safe, respectful, and constructive, regardless of experience, identity, background, employer, or point of view.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code, not personal characteristics.
- Give specific, actionable feedback and receive it in good faith.
- Protect confidential business data and security reports.
- Respect licenses, trademarks, authorship, and community boundaries.
- Stop behavior that a maintainer identifies as harmful or disruptive.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, deliberate disclosure of private information, sexualized conduct, sustained disruption, and publishing customer or credential data are not accepted.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the channel listed in the repository profile. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close participation, or ban contributors when necessary. They will handle reports confidentially where practical and will avoid conflicts of interest.

This policy applies in project repositories, reviews, issues, discussions, release channels, and public events where someone represents the project.
