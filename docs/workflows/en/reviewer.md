# Reviewer journey

1. Explicitly choose staged, unstaged, working-tree, or branch/base scope.
2. Inspect exclusions, renames, binaries, findings, callers, dependencies, and unresolved impact.
3. Navigate native Problems and compare JSON/SARIF by stable fingerprint.
4. Require external build/test proof for risks outside lexical analysis.
5. Record the decision with attribution; promote it only after reviewing the preview and hashes.

Outcome: a traceable decision with uncertainty and residual risk—not automatic approval because deterministic findings are absent.
